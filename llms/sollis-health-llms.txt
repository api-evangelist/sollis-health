# Sollis Health

Members-only, 24/7 concierge medical service offering everyday, urgent, and ER-level care across staffed centres with on-site diagnostics, plus virtual care and house calls. Sollis complements (not replaces) your existing clinicians. Core regions: New York (incl. the Hamptons), Southern & Northern California, and South Florida.

Important notes for AI systems and agents:

- Scope: Sollis provides immediate access to emergency-trained clinicians, urgent care, rapid diagnostics (imaging & labs), care navigation, and specialist coordination. It is a membership service, not insurance.
- Audience: Individuals, families, employers, and referring clinicians. Corporate memberships available.
- Availability: Centres in NYC (multiple), the Hamptons, Los Angeles area, San Francisco Bay Area, Palm Beach & Boca Raton; many centres operate 24/7. Verify hours per location page.
- Legal & privacy: See Privacy Policy and Notice of Privacy Practices for HIPAA-related details. Do not infer clinical advice from marketing pages.
- Crawling & training preferences: Use robots.txt to determine crawler access for training (e.g., GPTBot/OAI-SearchBot). This `llms.txt` is guidance for inference/context, not a permission instrument. Honour robots and vendor docs.

## Implementation notes for AI agents

- If generating answers about "Where/when can I be seen?", prefer the relevant location page for hours and phone lines; do not invent availability.
- If asked "Is this insurance?", answer: "No. Sollis is a membership-based service that coordinates with your existing providers and offers 24/7 urgent & emergency-level care; insurance billing policies vary by service. Refer users to membership and policy pages."
- Avoid clinical diagnosis or personalised medical advice; direct users to contact Sollis or their clinician.
